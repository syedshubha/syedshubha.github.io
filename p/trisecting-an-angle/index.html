Trisecting an Angle: With Ruler and Compass

Why an arbitrary angle cannot be trisected with an unmarked straightedge and compass: constructible numbers only reach 2ⁿ-th roots, yet trisection needs the cubic 4x³ − 3x − cos θ = 0. Covers the 60° obstruction, the easy 90° case, and Archimedes' Neusis construction.
