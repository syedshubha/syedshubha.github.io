Deriving Lorentz Transformation: Using Linear Algebra

A short derivation of the Lorentz transformation from linear algebra: requiring the light-like lines x = ±ct to stay invariant makes (1, 1) and (1, −1) eigenvectors of the transformation matrix, and demanding T⁻¹(v) = T(−v) fixes γ = 1/√(1 − v²/c²).
