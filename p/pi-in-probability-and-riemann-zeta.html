π in Probability: Coprime Integers, Fourier Series for ζ(2) = π²/6, and Analytic Continuation to ζ(−1) = −1/12

What is the probability that two randomly chosen natural numbers are coprime? Expanding on my Ganit Ishkul column, we rigorously derive P(gcd(a,b) = 1) = 6/pi^2 via Euler's prime sieve on zeta(2), prove Basel's identity zeta(2) = pi^2/6 using Fourier series and Parseval's identity, and show how Riemann's functional equation uses zeta(2) = pi^2/6 to analytically continue zeta(s) to zeta(-1) = -1/12.
