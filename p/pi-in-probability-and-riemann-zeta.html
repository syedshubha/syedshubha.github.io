π in Probability: Coprime Integers, Parseval's Identity for ζ(2) = π²/6, and Analytic Continuation to ζ(−1) = −1/12

What is the probability that two randomly chosen natural numbers are coprime? Expanding on my Ganit Ishkul column, we derive P(gcd(a, b) = 1) = 6/π² via Euler's prime sieve on ζ(2), prove ζ(2) = π²/6 in a few lines using Parseval's identity, and give an intuitive guide to analytic continuation showing how ζ(2) = π²/6 yields ζ(−1) = −1/12.
