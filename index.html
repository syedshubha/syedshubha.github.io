Syed Emad Uddin Shubha

Ph.D. Student in Computer Science • Louisiana State University

My doctoral research at Louisiana State University (advised by Dr. Tasnuva Farheen), which I conceived and proposed, spans two complementary directions: (1) Quantum Hardware Security: securing multi-tenant superconducting quantum processors via pulse-level crosstalk characterization, Pauli-transfer-matrix detection that separates malicious interference from benign calibration drift, and cross-layer verification; and (2) Quantum Communication & Quantum Correlations: exploiting quantum correlations to design adaptive error-correction and purification protocols and high-fidelity multi-qubit state transmission over partially entangled GHZ channels with optimal POVMs.
